This project is an independent daily-frequency adaptation of the diffusion controller used in:

Yu-Hao Huang, Chang Xu, Yang Liu, Weiqing Liu, Wu-Jun Li and Jiang Bian, *Controllable Financial Market Generation with Diffusion Guided Meta Agent*, AAAI 2026.

The neural networks and diffusion core derive from Microsoft/TimeCraft (MIT), pinned at 9a81eeef4f8487353a4aedb0b80b8a534e765720. Their headers credit lucidrains/denoising-diffusion-pytorch, copyright (c) 2020 Phil Wang (MIT). Original copyright notices and license texts are retained in LICENSE and third_party/licenses.

The daily data pipeline, portable experiment interfaces, channel-wise sampling bounds and selected result presentation are adaptations in this repository. Neither the source paper nor its authors endorse this independent implementation.
